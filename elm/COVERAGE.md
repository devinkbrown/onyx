## Known Elm-representation divergences

- **u32 epochs**: Elm `Int` is 32-bit signed; wire u32 encode/decode
  round-trips exactly (two's-complement wrap is consistent), but values
  `2^31..2^32-1` are unrepresentable as positive literals. Validators accept
  `0..maxInt`. Epochs increment from 0 in practice; recorded here, not hidden.
- **`String.length` vs UTF-8 bytes**: Elm has no UTF-8 primitive; byte lengths
  use a local UTF-8 encoder (`Base64Url.utf8ByteLength`, lone surrogates →
  U+FFFD like `TextEncoder`). UTF-16-unit counts (TS `.length`) need an
  explicit counter (`EventReplay.utf16Length`).
- **32-bit kernel traps** (verified against vendored `elm/core` kernel):
  `truncate` and `(//)` wrap quotients at 32 bits (`| 0`); `floor`,
  `modBy` (`%`), and `*`/`+` keep full double precision. Millisecond
  timestamps and base36 mints use `floor`/Float math, never `truncate`
  or large-operand `(//)`. U32 wire words use `Bitwise` (wrap intended).
- **Crypto**: Ed25519, AES-GCM, ECDH, SCRAM, WebAuthn ceremonies live behind
  ports. Elm owns all validation, parsing, packing, transcripts, and
  fail-closed display rules.
