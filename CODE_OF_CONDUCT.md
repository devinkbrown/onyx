# Code of conduct

Onyx is an open-source project and a shared technical space. Participants are
expected to be respectful, specific, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Discuss code, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking people.
- Keep reports reproducible and protect private data.
- Accept good-faith corrections and make room for quieter contributors.
- Respect the security-reporting process for vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized abuse, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, credential disclosure, and publishing private
security details are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the private
security/contact channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), without
including exploit details unless the report is also a security issue. Reports
will be reviewed in good faith. Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove
contributions and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project
safe and useful.
