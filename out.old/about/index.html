What is Ocean?

Built on IRC. Beyond IRC.

Ocean is the modern web client for the eshmaki.me IRC network. It brings the communication features you expect from modern platforms — voice, reactions, rich media, threads — while staying faithful to the open IRC protocol underneath.

Everything runs on Ophion, a custom IRC server built from the ground up to support modern clients without abandoning IRC compatibility. Any standard IRC client can still connect. Ocean is just the best way to experience the network.

The Technology

Four layers, one network.

IRC Servereshmaki.me:6697
Ophion
A custom IRC server built for performance and modern protocol extensions. Supports IRCv3, IRCX, CHATHISTORY, and full SASL auth including session tokens.
View source ↗
Media ProtocolNative transport · No relay
LADON
Proprietary voice and video protocol built over IRC messaging. Native transport — no relay servers, no third-party infrastructure. Spatial audio and video delivered through the Ophion network.
EncryptionP-256 ECDH · AES-256-GCM
VEIL
P-256 ECDH key exchange with AES-256-GCM encryption for every LADON session. Group session keys derived with forward secrecy — no plaintext voice leaves your device.
Web Clientopen source
Ocean
Next.js static export with Zustand state management. Dark luxury design system, bento layouts, and a full-featured IRC client under the hood.
Principles

Why we built this.

01
Open protocol
IRC is 35+ years old for good reason. Federated, simple, and proven. We extend it — we don't replace it.
02
No third parties
Voice goes through Ophion, not Google, not Amazon. Your conversations don't pass through infrastructure you don't control.
03
Privacy by design
Session tokens replace stored passwords. VEIL encrypts voice before it leaves your device. No analytics, no tracking.
04
Actually good software
Not "good for IRC". Just good. Spatial audio, live reactions, collaborative whiteboard, rich embeds — done right.
